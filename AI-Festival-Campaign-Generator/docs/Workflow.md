# Workflow

1. User fills the campaign form.

2. Campaign information is parsed.

3. Agent 1 generates festival intelligence.

4. Agent 2 creates marketing assets.

5. Agent 3 generates marketing visuals.

6. Images are uploaded to Google Drive.

7. Agent 4 reviews the campaign.

8. Campaign is logged into Google Sheets.

9. Campaign Kit Email is sent.

10. Business Summary Email is sent.
